About Fretwork

Built while learning, not after

Fretwork started as a personal practice log and grew into a small resource guide for other beginners working through the same early stumbling blocks.

Why This Exists

Every chord chart here was learned the slow way.

There's no shortage of guitar tutorials online, but most assume a level of comfort a total beginner doesn't have yet. Fretwork is written from the other side of that gap — the chords, order, and pacing that actually made sense while starting from zero, without any prior music background.
